# Version Roadmap

## Active release status

v1.00 is **Incomplete / Needs Work**. The immediate goal is a good-looking playable MVP: modeled water islands, intentional machine visuals, readable desktop/mobile UI, audio, balanced production and reliable saves. The user-approved catalog is eight droppers and eight sellers per rarity plus existing upgraders. Read `v1.00/MVP-Audit.md` and `v1.00/MVP-Completion-Plan.md`. v2.00 logistics remain planned until deployed MVP acceptance; historical branch names do not establish product completion.

## v1.00 — Core Factory Validation

**Core question:** Is building a factory from random machines fun?

**Scalability leap:** Tiny Factory gains a reliable single-route factory simulation, server-authoritative economy, machine inventory, rolling, saving, analytics, and a small reusable machine architecture.

**Major player-facing improvement:** The complete first playable factory loop.

**Major technical improvement:** A deterministic, maintainable simulation that can safely run persistent factories.

**Reason this version exists:** Prove the product before investing in complexity.

### Planned phases
1. Factory Simulation Foundation
2. Building & Placement
3. Rolling, Inventory & Economy
4. Launch Machine Set & Synergies
5. Persistence, Analytics & Security
6. Release Polish & Validation

---

## v2.00 — Factory Complexity & Content Scalability

**Core question:** Can players build meaningfully different factories?

**Scalability leap:** The factory evolves from one simple route into a modular logistics system with multiple lines, branching, merging, richer machines, better build tools, and stronger instrumentation.

**Major player-facing improvement:** Factories become genuine optimization puzzles rather than a single ordered row.

**Major technical improvement:** New machines and logistics nodes can be added without rewriting central systems.

**Reason this version exists:** Expand the decision space only after players prove they enjoy rebuilding.

### Planned phases
1. Logistics Graph Foundation
2. Branching, Splitters & Mergers
3. Machine Framework Expansion
4. Advanced Building, Inventory & Stats
5. Balance, Performance & v2 Validation

---

## v3.00 — Progression & Social Scalability

**Core question:** Can players develop long-term goals and meaningfully compare or interact with other factories?

**Scalability leap:** Adds long-term progression models and low-risk social layers without making multiplayer mandatory.

**Major player-facing improvement:** Collections, research, challenges, records, visiting/showcases, and optional cooperative or competitive goals where justified.

**Major technical improvement:** Persistent progression is separated cleanly from factory simulation; social reads are secure and low-coupling.

**Reason this version exists:** Give validated factory play longer-term structure.

### Planned phases
1. Meta-Progression Foundation
2. Collection, Research & Challenges
3. Factory Showcase & Visiting
4. Records, Leaderboards & Optional Social Goals
5. Long-Term Progression Validation

---

## v4.00 — Live Service & Content Pipeline Scalability

**Core question:** Can one developer efficiently operate and expand Tiny Factory over the long term?

**Scalability leap:** Machine content, events, configuration, validation, migrations, debugging, observability, and rollout controls become safer and cheaper.

**Major player-facing improvement:** More reliable content cadence and limited-time variations without destabilizing the core game.

**Major technical improvement:** Data-driven content pipeline, automated validators, feature flags, migration tooling, admin/debug tools, and balance simulation.

**Reason this version exists:** Reduce the cost and risk of continued development.

### Planned phases
1. Data-Driven Content Pipeline
2. Developer Tools & Automated Validation
3. Feature Flags & Live Configuration
4. Data Reliability, Migrations & Observability
5. Seasonal Content Pilot & Operations Validation

---
